Hello!

Hi, I'm Isadora Vieira — a front-end developer from São Paulo, Brazil. My background blends creativity and technology; I've explored video editing, digital content, and illustration, which now fuel my passion for building clean, functional web interfaces. I started coding at 17 and recently returned to front-end development with renewed focus. Currently, I'm deepening my knowledge in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through hands-on projects. I use YouTube and ChatGPT as learning tools, dedicating consistent hours each day to studying and building real applications. For now, my focus is entirely on front-end development. My short-term goal is to secure my first remote (Home Office) opportunity as a Junior Front-end Developer, where I can contribute, learn from experienced teams, and grow professionally. You might know me online as Kuromi — a little Hello Kitty-inspired twist to my digital presence. Feel free to explore my projects on GitHub or connect with me here. Let's build something great together!
